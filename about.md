### Hello, I'm Kerry

I'm a software developer based in Cheshire, UK. I've been writing code professionally for over 18 years, mostly in the .NET ecosystem, though I'm currently picking up Ruby on Rails as part of my day job.

Before I got into software, I spent a decade as an electrical engineer at British Steel. That background still shapes how I approach problems - I think of debugging code the same way I used to trace faults in industrial control systems.

These days I lead a small team building software for the UK holiday park industry. I get particular satisfaction from making slow things fast - there's something deeply rewarding about taking a system that grinds to a halt and making it fly.

Outside of work, I enjoy tinkering with home automation (Home Assistant runs most of my house), mechanical keyboards, and finding ways to automate the tedious bits of daily computing. This blog is where I write about whatever I'm currently figuring out.

You can find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/KerryWalker) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kerry-w-8180ba13a/).
